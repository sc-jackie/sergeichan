# The Loom — material study (Silk chosen, 5 alternative line materials)

**Date:** 2026-07-10 · **Status:** Sergei chose the Silk/Loom direction (D10); this study varies
only the *material* the lines are made of. Architecture is locked: one continuous line-surface is
the whole site (hero, dividers, billow transitions, reading ribbon), per-project variants, day-silk
light reading pages for articles, scroll-only interaction.
**Live previews:** claude.ai artifact "The Loom — five line materials" (M0 silk reference + M1–M5).

## M0 · Silk (reference — the pick)
Woven threads, indigo ground, iridescent crest. Sensuous, tactile, slow.

## M1 · Light Beams
Lines as volumetric shafts of warm white light through darkness (cathedral effect); dust motes;
scroll = solar azimuth sweeping the beams; content lives where light falls. Two colors total.
Per-project: beam configurations (crons = parallel thin beams, Newfin = one wide slit beam, etc.).
*Pick if:* awe — the abstract heir to the Medici instinct.

## M2 · Aurora
Lines as chromatic curtains of northern light over a starfield; scroll = wind (curtains lean with
velocity). Only multi-color option (green/ion blue/violet, saturation-capped). Strongest bridge to
the travel/awe essays. *Pick if:* atmosphere and color, least "tech portfolio."

## M3 · Neon Wire
Lines as energized conductors — crisp PCB-style traces, bright core + glow, pulses traveling like
signals; pulses can be **true telemetry** (cron firings). One cyan, mono typography. Lightest GPU
after strings. Cliché risk (Tron) managed by restraint + honest data. *Pick if:* precision +
live-data honesty.

## M4 · Light Trails
Lines painted by moving particles — long-exposure streaks (accumulation buffer); scroll bends the
flow field, clearing calm space around the text; idle = embers. Warm black + amber streaks.
*Pick if:* the site should feel alive at every moment.

## M5 · Strings
Lines as taut instrument strings — still until touched; pointer plucks, scroll strums, sections
ring as chords (damped-oscillator physics). Pearl strings + one gold. Effectively free to render —
cheapest of all. Per-project = chords (string count/spacing/decay). *Pick if:* restraint with a
secret; most interactive, most performant, nobody else has it.

Materials can blend at the edges (silk crest lighting up like aurora; strings leaving faint trails)
but one must lead.
