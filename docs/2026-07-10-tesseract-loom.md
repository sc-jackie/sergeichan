# The Tesseract Loom — the unified direction

**Date:** 2026-07-10 · **Status:** Direction locked in concept: **Silk × string theory × Interstellar
tesseract**. Live interactive concept: claude.ai artifact "The Tesseract Loom — silk × string theory".
Loom architecture unchanged (one line-surface = whole site, per-project variants, day-silk light
reading pages, billow transitions, scroll-only).

## Site thesis (one sentence)

> Everything here is one thread — five systems, the essays, two cultures — vibrating in different modes.

## The three ingredients

- **From silk — the material.** Threads keep silk's weight and ease; pearl on deep space; no neon.
  Iridescent crest for transitions only; pressed dim zones under text; day-silk articles unchanged.
- **From string theory — the metaphor.** One thread, different **vibration modes** = different
  projects (Mode I Jackie-OS fundamental · II Newfin, the still node = balance point · III B-Unit,
  ringing up · IV Rodyna, warm detune · V Draw, quick + crisp). Real standing-wave physics, shown
  on every case study.
- **From the tesseract — the space + the touch.** Threads live in a 3D depth lattice; **scroll =
  travel through depth planes** (sections are planes; a right-edge depth gauge is the nav). The
  signature interaction is **pulling strings**: drag bends the near-layer threads (spring k≈0.06,
  damping 0.88), release ripples to neighbors. One gold "gravity" thread carries a traveling pulse;
  its dash texture is Morse for **STAY** (quiet Easter egg).

## Iteration 2 (same day) — home ↔ case-study prototype

Sergei feedback: hero too messy; projects should live *in* the threads with hover-glimpses that
deepen the longer you hover ("rabbithole"); click = Netflix-style dive transition into the case
study; case studies need full content (what/why/solving/state/looks/architecture); scroll
choreography deferred — scope locked to **home + case studies + transitions**.

Working prototype shipped (claude.ai artifact "Tesseract Loom — home ↔ case-study prototype"):
- **Cleaner hero:** 3 depth layers (was 5), ~60% fewer threads, no lattice grid, slower sway;
  background threads part away from the pointer; five emphasized **project threads** with labels.
- **Hover rabbithole:** nearest project thread turns gold + gravity bead; glimpse card stages at
  0.2s (name) / 0.8s (one-liner + wireframe glimpse) / 1.6s (meta, camera leans in, background
  fades) / 2.4s (enter-ring filled); decays on leave.
- **Dive transition:** click → camera plunges into the thread (zoom ×9 + radial streaks) while the
  glimpse card expands fullscreen → case study fades up (~0.9s total). Esc / "Resurface" reverses.
- **Case-study template, all five fully written:** what it is · why it exists · what it solves ·
  current state (chips) · how it looks (wireframe frames as screenshot stand-ins) · architecture
  (node chain + note) · next-thread link; gold reading-progress line; privacy boundary respected
  (no family names, no real figures).
- Mobile: tap = glimpse (staged instantly), tap card = dive. Reduced-motion: instant switch.
