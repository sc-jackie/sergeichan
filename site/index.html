ABOUT

Sergei Tran

I trained as a designer and worked in UI/UX from 2014 to 2018, from startups to Lexus, Suzuki and Toyota dealer sites in Singapore. I then moved into blockchain research and community work, followed by a long run as a full-time investor.

Now I build AI-native systems in Warsaw: five products in production, run day to day with always-on agents. One is an operating system for my life; the others run on its rails.

I am Vietnamese-Ukrainian, born in Odesa and raised between two languages. My writing follows that thread.
