The position

Balance isn’t liquidity.

A transfer isn’t spending.

A bot shouldn’t become a second ledger.

Every finance app tracks a slice. None could hold the whole book without lying somewhere — vesting counted as cash, a transfer counted as spending, property with no place at all. Newfin exists to hold it all, truthfully.

The line that matters

Liquid ≠ Locked

LIQUID crypto · cash · trading
LOCKED vesting · property · deals

Both belong in net worth. Only one is money you can move today — so Newfin never adds them into one flattering number.

What it solves

Built for the book
no app could hold.

One truthful net-worth line

Every asset class, every currency, one calculation — with liquid and locked kept apart so the number you can move is never inflated.

An advisor with numbers

It reads a scoped snapshot of the real book and answers in dollars and percent — never market clichés.

Hard household scoping

The owner sees everything. Family members see only their view — enforced before the model runs.

Read-only by design

It can describe a move. It can never execute one. No keys, no orders, no transfers.

A live desk beside the book

IBKR equities and Hyperliquid perps on one desk — so a new position is sized against everything you own, not one account’s margin screen.
