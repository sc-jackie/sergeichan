What it holds

The book, in three columns

Newfin keeps one source of truth for net worth, spending, and live risk — wired to the venues the household actually uses.

Portfolio

Positions & net worth

Buckets for crypto, cash, property, private equity, receivables, and trading accounts — liquid vs locked called out so OPM and vesting don’t fake the liquid number.

Cashflow

Spend & income

Category books, ether.fi cash reconcile, budgets, and retention jobs that run on the VPS schedule instead of fighting Vercel Hobby cron limits.

Persik

Telegram COO

@Persik_finbot answers day/week money questions, syncs vault ops for the owner, and routes research through the same Newfin API the web app uses.

Live desk

IBKR and Hyperliquid on one board

The trading desk consolidates venue accounts, open positions, and exposure — Flex pulls for equities, perps marks when the session is off-hours. Sync from the app or the four-hour VPS cron.
