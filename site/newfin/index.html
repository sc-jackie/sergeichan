Private by design. Built around one household, not a public signup funnel.

Your financial life doesn’t live in one system.

Broker accounts settle on their own clock. Cards describe purchases differently from wallets. Property and private equity have no live feed at all. A single balance can look precise while hiding what is liquid, stale, borrowed, or locked.

Newfin exists to reconcile those disagreements into one inspectable household book — without pretending unlike assets are the same thing.

Balance isn’t liquidity.
Vesting, property, receivables, and OPM belong in net worth, but not in the number you can move today.
A transfer isn’t spending.
Moving cash between a Safe, a card, and a trading venue should not become three expenses.
A bot shouldn’t become a second ledger.
Persik reads the same scoped state as the web app, so a Telegram answer cannot drift into its own version of the truth.

For a household with more moving parts than a bank app can explain.

Newfin is not trying to be a mass-market budgeting app. It is an operating layer for one technically hands-on household whose assets, spending, and decisions cross systems.

Owner
The complete book, advisor context, trading surfaces, operations, and private integrations.
Spouse
Household-scoped balances, cashflow, and answers — without leaking owner-only routes or integrations.
Family group
Shared questions and useful household context through Persik, constrained before any model call.

One ledger in the middle. Different surfaces around it.

The app stays understandable because ingestion, financial truth, AI context, and delivery are separate layers.

Read-only AI
Models reason over a scoped FinancialState payload. They do not receive signing keys and cannot move money.
Role before model
Owner, spouse, and group scope is applied when the payload is assembled — not after an answer is generated.
Portable by default
React, Node, Postgres, and a versioned API keep the system self-hostable instead of coupling the book to one interface.

The system doesn’t stop at the browser.

Persik handles the quick question. The Hermes VPS keeps background syncs, research, and scheduled work moving while the laptop is closed.
