One household. One book. No second ledger.

Wealth doesn’t live in one app — but decisions have to.

Real household money is scattered on purpose: IBKR, Hyperliquid, hot and cold wallets, cards in two currencies, an apartment that isn’t sellable this month, a vesting grant that won’t unlock for years, money someone still owes you. Every dashboard is confident it’s showing “your net worth.”

None of them are. A chart total isn’t what you can move on Tuesday. Newfin exists because a household needs one book that knows the difference, respects who’s allowed to see what, and answers the boring question fast: what do we actually have, right now, that we can use?

Scattered ≠ diversified.

How it works

Five steps from scattered accounts to one book you can ask from the phone.

  1. 01

    Connect sources

    Point Newfin at the accounts that already exist — exchanges, wallets, IBKR, Hyperliquid, cards, cash pots, property values, receivables, OPM. Connections are read-only where possible, and manual where honest. Nothing gets a signing key.

    • On-chain wallets alongside cards and cash
    • Brokerages and trading venues (IBKR, Hyperliquid)
    • Property, receivables, and other-people’s-money in the same book
  2. 02

    Reconcile into one book

    Newfin folds those sources into one household ledger, matching transfers against spend so a wallet-to-exchange move stops looking like income and an exchange-to-card withdrawal stops looking like a purchase. Scheduled syncs keep it current without babysitting.

    • Transfers detected and cancelled against spend
    • Cards + on-chain cash treated as one cash surface
    • Scheduled background syncs, not on-demand panic
  3. 03

    See liquid vs locked

    Every position is tagged by what it actually is: liquid, locked, vesting, or illiquid. Net worth is one number; spendable is a different one — both shown, neither hidden inside the other.

    • Vesting kept out of “spendable”
    • Property valued but flagged illiquid
    • Receivables and OPM separated from own capital
  4. 04

    Check cashflow & trading in context

    Cashflow is the household’s real burn and inflow across every card and wallet. The live desk shows open positions on IBKR and Hyperliquid next to that cushion — so a bigger position is a decision against real liquidity, not a vibe.

    • Household cashflow across cards + on-chain
    • Open positions from IBKR and Hyperliquid
    • Position size read against real liquid cushion
  5. 05

    Ask Persik from Telegram

    Persik is the same book, over Telegram. Ask what’s liquid, what came in this month, what a position is worth — from your phone. Persik sees exactly what your role allows, and cannot move money.

    • Same backend as the web app — not a copy
    • Role scoping applied before the model sees anything
    • Read-only: no keys, no trades, no transfers

Plain-language stack.

A single household backend with two front doors. The web PWA and Persik both talk to the same Postgres, through the same role rules — so there is no second ledger to drift.

Read-only AI
No signing keys. Persik and any model can read the household — they cannot move money.
Role before model
Scoping is applied when the payload is assembled — not after an answer is generated. Access isn’t a prompt instruction; it’s a boundary.
Portable by default
One household, one book, exportable. A private ledger you happen to run on these surfaces — not a walled garden.
